About the Yeshiva

In Sivan תשנ״ח Maran Harav Aharon Yehuda Leib Steinman zt"l founded the yeshiva, and on Rosh Chodesh Elul its beis medrash opened in a rented building at 26 Rechov Ben Yaakov, with thirty bochurim and a single shiur. At the opening Maran defined its purpose: to raise gedolei Torah for the generations to come.

Maran placed his talmidim Harav Baruch Dov Diskin and Harav Itamar Garbuz at the head of the yeshiva. In the summer of תשנ״ט Harav Yechiel Kalerman joined them, and in the summer of תש״ס Harav Tzvi Yehuda Edelstein. Maran himself said a shiur klali there every week, and said of it: "Orchos Torah is my ben zekunim."

Year after year the yeshiva grew: from its first building to one on Rechov Harav Kahaneman, and on Chanukah תשס״ד to the campus on Rechov Shlomo Hamelech, whose beis medrash seats more than five hundred today, beside a fourteen-story dormitory.

Customs that spread across the yeshiva world began here too, like the learning seder on Purim night, set in the yeshiva at Maran's instruction in תשנ״ט and adopted over the years by most yeshivos.
