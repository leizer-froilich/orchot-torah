One Home of Torah, Across Eretz Yisroel

וְהָגִיתָ בּוֹ יוֹמָם וָלַיְלָה“And you shall toil in it day and night” · Yehoshua 1:8

In Sivan תשנ״ח (1998), Maran Harav Aharon Yehuda Leib Steinman zt"l founded Yeshivas Orchos Torah in Bnei Brak. Since then the yeshiva has grown into a network of yeshivos gedolos, yeshivos ketanos and some twenty kollelim, from the Galil to the Negev.

In every beis medrash of the network, the derech Maran set out is carefully kept: learning b'shketus and with anavah, with no competition between talmidim, out of ahavas Torah and yiras Shamayim.

Maran Harav Steinman zt"l

Founder of the Yeshiva · תרע״ד - תשע״ח

Among the leaders of the generation and the greatest marbitzei Torah. In his youth he learned in Yeshivas Toras Chesed in Brisk, and he built Orchos Torah as a home where every bochur can grow in Torah quietly, with anavah and l'shem Shamayim.

At his instruction, and unlike the common practice, the shiurim in the yeshiva were never divided by age, so that there would be no competition between talmidim. Maran came to the yeshiva many times and said shiurim there, and to this day the entire network carries his derech.

Roshei Yeshiva: Harav Itamar Garbuz · Harav Baruch Dov Diskin · Harav Yechiel Kalerman · Harav Tzvi Yehuda Edelstein
Nasi HaYeshiva: Harav Shraga Noach Steinman

The Kollel Network

From a Small Beginning
to a Network of Torah

תשנ״ח1998

Maran Harav Steinman founds the yeshiva in Bnei Brak, in a temporary building on Rechov Harav Kahaneman, and places his talmidim at its head.

תש״ע2010

A branch of the yeshiva opens in Ashdod, for bochurim from Sephardic communities.

תשע״א - תשע״ב2011 - 2012

Rinah Shel Torah opens in Karmiel, and Toras Chesed in Elad, named for the yeshiva in Brisk where Maran learned in his youth.

תשע״ג - תשע״ד2013 - 2014

Yeshivas Ner Zerach joins the network and moves to Moshav Otzem, and Yeshivas Torah B'Tifartah opens its doors.

תשע״ו - תשע״ח2016 - 2017

Imrei Moshe in Yerushalayim, named for the sefer of Maran's rebbi. During the shivah after his petirah, the cornerstone is laid for the "Ayeles HaShachar" campus.

תשפ״ב and on2022

Romemus HaTorah opens in Bnei Brak. Today 4,500 bochurim and some 1,300 avreichim learn in the network.
