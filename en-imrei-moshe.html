About the Yeshiva

Yeshivas Imrei Moshe was established in תשע״ו, the last yeshiva Maran founded in his lifetime. Maran named it for his rebbi, Harav Moshe Sokolovsky, the Imrei Moshe, who left no children, and said that through the yeshiva the pasuk is fulfilled in him: "I will give them in My house and within My walls a place and a name, better than sons and daughters."

At the founding gathering in his home, on 10 Nissan תשע״ו, Maran said that thousands would yet go out from this place, and that from here Torah would go forth to Klal Yisroel.

Maran instructed that the emphasis be on toil in Torah, making use of time, and middos tovos, and these remain the yeshiva's hallmarks to this day. Today 214 talmidim learn there.
