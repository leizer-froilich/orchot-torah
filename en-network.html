One Home of Torah, Across Eretz Yisroel

וְהָגִיתָ בּוֹ יוֹמָם וָלַיְלָה"And you shall toil in it day and night" · Yehoshua 1:8

In Sivan תשנ״ח (1998), Maran Harav Aharon Yehuda Leib Steinman zt"l founded Yeshivas Orchos Torah in Bnei Brak. Since then the yeshiva has grown into a whole network: yeshivos gedolos, yeshivos ketanos and some twenty kollelim.

Each institution is a home of its own, and together they carry one derech. A bochur can grow within the network from his first years in yeshiva through his years in kollel, without ever leaving the home Maran built.

Maran's Life Journey →

The Derech Set Out by Maran

Quietly, with Anavah

Maran wanted a home where every bochur can grow in Torah quietly, with anavah and l'shem Shamayim.

No Competition

At his instruction, and unlike the common practice, the shiurim were never divided by age, so that there would be no competition between talmidim.

A Close Framework

The network's yeshivos keep smaller vaadim than is common, at Maran's instruction, so that every bochur receives personal attention.

Every Bochur Is Precious

Maran taught that the heart of chinuch is personal example, and he opposed sending talmidim away from their institutions.
